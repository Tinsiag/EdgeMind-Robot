4.4.  Housing design guide 
Housing design follow as below 
 
1)  Avoid the design that housing overlap and press on the active area of the LCM 
 
2) Give enough gap(over 0.5mm at compressed) between the housing and TSP 
to   
          protect wrong operating. 
 
 
3)  Use a buffer material(Gasket) between the TSP and housing to protect 
damage and wrong operating 
 
          4)   Avoid the design that buffer material overlap and press on the inside of TSP 
view area.
